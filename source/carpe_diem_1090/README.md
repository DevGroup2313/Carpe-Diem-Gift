Build 1090 is made from build 1089 by `cd1090.py IN_1089.html OUT.html`, once for each edition. The parts:

* `p_gbprojects90.py`: **the demonstrations' Guidebook: their two chapters with names of their own, each in its
  section.** Build 1089 placed the Guidebook's chapters in sections by their English titles. In the two demonstrations
  two chapters are named otherwise: Projects (the owner's Candidatus) and Renaming for your writing (the owner's
  Renaming for your proposal); both fell into a tenth section, More chapters. They now stand in Your projects and in
  Reading and study. The owner's edition has no chapters of those names and is unchanged.
* `p_builders.py`: the install kits carry build 1090.

The two demonstrations were brought from 1081 to 1089 by running each pass from 1082 to 1089 on them. One edit was
left out in both, as it finds nothing to change there: 1082's printing through the Mac and Windows apps
(cdPrintViaPython), which only the owner's edition carries. Every other edit of 1082 to 1089 applied as written.

Tests: 1087's `t_rooms_face.js` and 1089's `t_guidebook.js` (with the demonstrations' entry card closed first) on both
demonstrations; the seal, the build record and Tabula on all three pages.
